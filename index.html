My dearest Lita,

Not a single hour passes that I do not think of you, baby.
I wake, and my first thought is you.
I sleep, and my last wish is you.

They say distance is cruel. Perhaps it is.
But it has taught me that no ocean is wide enough,
no map is long enough,
no clock is slow enough
to keep my heart away from yours.

I carry you with me like a secret only I know:
your smile on a tiny screen,
your voice at midnight,
your sleepy face saying goodnight.

Every day apart is one more day closer.
And when I hold you again,
I will not let go for a very long time.

Until then, a thousand kisses,
a thousand more when I see you,
and all of my heart, always.

Yours, only yours,

Dennis ♡
